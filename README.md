## About This System

The Intelligent-Voice-Driving-System monitors your state while you operate a vehicle. The software uses the Qwen-7B artificial intelligence model to track your alertness. It allows you to control vehicle functions through your voice. The system provides real-time feedback and maintains a conversation to keep you engaged. You require no manual input to use the features. The software manages the process from start to finish.

## 💻 System Requirements

To run this software smoothly on your Windows computer, you need the following hardware:

* Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit).
* Processor: Intel Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 or better.
* Memory: 16 GB of RAM.
* Storage: 20 GB of free space on a solid-state drive.
* Audio: A microphone connected to your system.
* Graphics: An NVIDIA graphics card with at least 8 GB of video memory.

## 🗣️ Using Voice Commands

The system listens for specific phrases to perform tasks. You do not need to press buttons while driving. Use these example commands:

* Start system: Tells the software to begin monitoring your fatigue levels.
* Report status: Provides a spoken summary of your current drive metrics.
* Adjust volume: Changes the audio level of the AI assistant.
* End session: Safely closes the connection and powers down the monitor.

## 🛡️ Safety Warnings

Always keep your eyes on the road. This software acts as an aid and does not replace your attention. Do not rely on the AI for emergency braking or steering. If the system detects signs of fatigue, find a safe place to stop your vehicle. Ensure your microphone is positioned away from wind noise or loud air conditioning vents to maintain accuracy.

## 🧩 Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the software fails to start, check the following items:

* Check your internet connection. The AI needs a connection to process voice data.
* Ensure no other programs are currently using your microphone.
* Restart your computer if the program stops responding.
* Update your graphics card drivers through the manufacturer website if the video feed feels slow.
* Verify that you have enough space on your hard drive for the AI model files.
